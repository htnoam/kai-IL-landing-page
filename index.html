About KAI

With 15+ years of luxury event production experience, We craft destination wedding experiences that feel deeply personal and completely seamless. We've learned that the best weddings aren't just beautiful — they're deeply connected to who you are.

What began as a passion for bringing visions to life has evolved into a mission: helping couples celebrate their love in the world's most beautiful locations without the stress. Every wedding We orchestrate reflects the couple's story, their values, and their dreams — because your day should feel uniquely yours, not generic.

We specialize in full destination planning — from Greece and Cyprus to Italy, France, and beyond. We take on a limited number of weddings each year to ensure every couple gets complete attention. My approach is simple: We listen deeply to understand your vision, We plan meticulously to handle every detail, and We execute flawlessly so you can simply enjoy the day.
