Destination Weddings

We specialize in creating unforgettable celebrations across Europe's most stunning locations. Each destination offers its own magic—from island sunsets to rolling vineyards—and we know how to capture it.

🇬🇷 Greece

Santorini · Mykonos · Crete · Athens

Iconic islands with breathtaking sunsets, whitewashed villages, and authentic Mediterranean charm. From cliff-side ceremonies to seaside receptions, Greece offers romance and adventure in equal measure.

Peak season: May–September

🇨🇾 Cyprus

Paphos · Limassol · Troodos Mountains

Mediterranean warmth and elegance with golden beaches, ancient history, and world-class hospitality. Cyprus offers the perfect blend of relaxation, adventure, and intimate celebration.

Peak season: April–October

🇮🇹 Italy

Tuscany · Amalfi Coast · Lake Como · Venice

Timeless romance in rolling vineyards, dramatic coastlines, and historic villas. Italy represents the pinnacle of elegance—perfect for couples seeking classic, unforgettable celebrations.

Peak season: May–September
