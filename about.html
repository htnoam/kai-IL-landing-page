About KAI

We craft destination weddings with heartfelt passion and meticulous precision. Every detail—from initial concept to final celebration—is orchestrated with care, allowing you to simply enjoy your wedding day surrounded by the people you love most.

Our Story

Founded by Noam Salach, KAI brings years of experience in destination wedding planning across Europe's most beautiful locations. What started as a vision to create unforgettable celebrations has grown into a trusted brand for couples seeking authenticity, creativity, and flawless execution.

We believe every couple's love story is unique. That's why we don't offer cookie-cutter packages—we craft personalized experiences that reflect your vision, values, and dreams.

Our Approach

Personal Partnership

We work alongside you from the very first conversation, understanding your vision and bringing it to life with creativity and care.

Attention to Detail

Every element—lighting, florals, logistics, guest experience—is carefully orchestrated to create a seamless, magical day.

Local Expertise

Our deep connections with European vendors, venues, and suppliers ensure access to the best and most authentic experiences.

Stress-Free Planning

Planning from abroad? No problem. We handle everything on the ground so you can stay relaxed and excited throughout the process.
