Our Services

We offer a complete approach to destination wedding planning, from initial concept through post-wedding follow-up. Whether you need full planning or day-of coordination, we craft every experience with heartfelt care.

Core Services

✨ Full Planning

Complete end-to-end wedding planning from your first conversation through your honeymoon send-off. We handle all logistics, vendor coordination, and creative direction.

Ideal for: Couples planning from abroad who want full peace of mind

🎯 Day-of Coordination

Focused coordination for your wedding day and nearby events. We manage timeline, vendor coordination, and real-time problem-solving so you stay present and relaxed.

Ideal for: Couples who've planned independently and want expert day-of support

🏰 Venue Sourcing

Curated venue discovery across Europe's most beautiful destinations. We scout locations, negotiate terms, and ensure the perfect backdrop for your celebration.

Ideal for: Couples who know their destination but need expert venue guidance

🎨 Décor & Design

Custom design and styling that brings your vision to life. From floral installations to lighting design, we create immersive experiences that reflect your personality.

Ideal for: Couples seeking a specific aesthetic or creative direction

👥 Guest Experience

Seamless accommodation coordination, welcome events, transportation, and group activities. We ensure your guests feel celebrated and cared for throughout their stay.

Ideal for: Destination weddings with multiple guest events
